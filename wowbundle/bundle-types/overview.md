# Bundle Types

Choose the tier type that fits how your products are sold.

| Type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [Volume](quantity-breaks.md) | Multiple units of the same product |
| [Buy X Get Y](buy-x-get-y.md) | Bought and received quantities with separate pricing controls |
| [Bundle](mixed-bundles.md) | Different products combined in one tier |

After choosing a type, review its products, quantities and discount fields. Add gifts or optional extras separately when needed.
