# Tier Upsells

Offer an optional extra with a specific tier rather than every choice in the bundle.

## Step 1: Open the tier

Expand the tier in **Offer** and enable its **Upsell** add-on.

## Step 2: Select the product

Choose the upsell product and review its discount and variant options. Keep the product published and available.

## Step 3: Save and check the choice

Save the offer. Select the tier, choose the add-on variant and select the upsell before adding to cart.

Check the cart with the upsell selected and unselected. Also switch to another tier and confirm that the optional product matches the customer's current choice.

## Step 4: Check linked items

Remove the parent product and review the linked upsell. Recheck the offer after changing the add-on's product options or availability.

This control belongs to an individual tier. For an optional choice configured under Extras, see [Checkbox Upsells](checkbox-upsells.md).
