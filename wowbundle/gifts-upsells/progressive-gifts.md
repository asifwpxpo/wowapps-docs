# Progressive Gifts

Add rewards at quantity thresholds to show customers what they can unlock next.

For example, offer one reward at a smaller quantity and a product gift at a larger quantity.

## Step 1: Open Progressive gifts

Go to **Offer → Extras → Progressive gifts**.

## Step 2: Configure the rewards

Review the threshold and reward controls. Choose any required gift products and update the reward copy to match your offer.

## Step 3: Check each threshold

Save the offer. Add quantities just below and at each threshold. Then reduce the quantity in cart and verify the reward still matches eligibility.

For a shipping reward, check the actual checkout shipping charge with an eligible destination. A free shipping label alone does not confirm how the shipping rate will change.
