
# App Introduction

Grow your sales and keep customers coming back by showing clear estimated delivery dates with WPXPO’s Estimated Delivery Date (EDD) app for Shopify. This tool makes it easy to display estimated shipping dates, preparation times, and delivery timelines. With ready-to-use templates, you can customize preparation times, delivery dates, and even add personalized shipping messages. You can set estimated arrival times (ETA) for different countries and manage shipping schedules by defining working hours, cut-off times, and vacation periods to streamline operations and reduce cart abandonment on your Shopify store.

### What You Can Do:

- Show estimated delivery times (EDD/ETA) based on selected products, no coding needed.
- Customize the look of ETA messages with pre-designed templates and simple shortcodes on Shopify.
- Set specific delivery dates for different countries.
- Manage shipping with flexible working hours, cut-off times, and vacation notices.
- Use translations and analytics to optimize your delivery process.
- You can create delivery date estimates for specific countries, with or without dedicated ETA timings
- You can use the countdown timer to highlight ‘limited-time’ offers on your store
- You can show the delivery notification on the checkout page, cart drawer, order status page, and thank you page.
