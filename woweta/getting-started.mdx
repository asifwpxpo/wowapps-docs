# Getting Started

## WowETA Shopify App Installation:

WowETA can be installed directly from the Shopify App Store or from your Shopify store admin dashboard.

Here’s the TL;DR:

1. Log in to your Shopify store dashboard
2. Search for the WowETA App
3. Select the App and review the details
4. Click on Install
5. Complete the installation from your Shopify Store Dashboard
6. Upgrade/downgrade based on your requirements

## Installing the WowETA Shopify App

Follow these steps to install an app directly from the Shopify App Store:

### Log in to the Shopify App Store

Visit [apps.shopify.com](https://apps.shopify.com) or go to your Shopify admin, click **Apps** in the left-hand menu.

Furthermore, if you click on **_'Apps'_** from your dashboard, you can either click on **_"All Recommended Apps"_** or **_"App and Sales Channel Settings"_**. You can visit the Shopify App Store directly from both these settings.

![WowETA App Store](/docs/woweta-app-store.png)

### Find the WowETA App

Browse categories or use the search bar to locate the WowETA app.

Review the app’s description, ratings, and compatibility to ensure it suits your store’s needs.

### Select the App

Click on the app’s listing to view its details, including features, pricing, and requirements.

### Install the WowETA App on Your Shopify Store

Since you’re logged in, you’ll find the “Install” button for the WowETA app (or any app you inspect more deeply).

If you click on “Install”, you’ll be taken to your dashboard.

### Authorize the App

You’ll be redirected to your Shopify admin dashboard to review the WowETA app’s permissions. Click **Install app** to grant access and complete the installation.

### Configure the App

After installation, WowETA will appear in your Shopify admin under **Apps**. Open it to customize settings or preferences to match your store’s requirements.

_You can also go to ‘Apps > Apps and Sales Channels’ and find all your Shopify apps listed there. From here, you can choose to get support, view details of the WowETA app, and more._

### App Subscription Upgrade

To upgrade to the pro version of the app, you need to go to the “Pricing Plan” section of the app. From there, you can upgrade or downgrade based on your preference. Pricing will be adjusted accordingly based on your use.

![WowETA Pricing Plan](/docs/woweta-pricing-plan.png)

You can get support for this Shopify app by emailing [support@wpxpo.com](mailto:support@wpxpo.com) directly or clicking on Resources > Get Support from the WowETA plugin dashboard.
