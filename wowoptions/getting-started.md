# Getting Started

Getting started with WowOptions takes only a few minutes. Follow the steps below to install the app and prepare your store for advanced product customization.

## Install WowOptions

You can install WowOptions directly from the Shopify App Store or from your Shopify admin dashboard. Follow these Quick Steps:

1. Log in to your Shopify store
2. Go to the Apps section
3. Open the Shopify App Store
4. Search for **W: Custom Product Options**
5. Click Install
6. Confirm installation

Follow the step-by-step installation guide below.

## Step 1: Log in to your Shopify Store

Go to [Shopify.com](https://shopify.com) and sign in to your store using your admin credentials.

Make sure you are using:

- A Store Owner account, or
- A Staff account with app installation permissions

Without proper permissions, you won't be able to install the app.

## Step 2: Find the WowOptions App

From your Shopify admin:

1. Go to Apps from the left sidebar
2. Click Shopify App Store
3. Search for: WowOptions: Custom Product Options
4. Select the app from the results to open its listing page. Here you can review features, pricing, and details.

## Step 3: Install the App

1. Click Install on the app listing page
2. Review the permissions
3. Click Install app to confirm
4. Once installed, WowOptions will appear in your Shopify admin under Apps.

## Open & Configure the App

After installation:

- Go to Apps → WowOptions
- Open the app to start configuring your product options
- You can customize settings, create option fields, and adjust behavior based on your store's needs.

## Manage the App

You can also manage WowOptions from:

- Apps → Apps and Sales Channels

From here, you can:

- View app details
- Manage permissions
- Access support

## Next Steps

To get the most out of WowOptions:

- Explore the Settings page
- Learn how to use Option Fields
- Set up Conditional Logic
- Check the FAQ for common issues and solutions

You're now ready to start building powerful product customization experiences!
